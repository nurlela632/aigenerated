A useful image prompt begins with a clear subject

An image prompt is a description of what you want a visual system to create. The strongest prompts usually explain the subject and purpose before adding decorative details. A clear description gives the model a better starting point than a long list of unrelated adjectives.

For example, a prompt for a website hero image might describe a person working at a desk with a laptop, a calm office environment, and open space for a headline. That is more actionable than simply requesting a beautiful professional technology image.

Describe composition and viewpoint

Composition determines where the viewer's attention goes. Mention whether you want a close-up, a wide scene, a centered subject, or empty space on one side. You can also describe the camera angle and the relationship between foreground and background.

If the image will appear beside website text, request a composition that leaves room for that text. If it will be used as a small thumbnail, favor a simple subject and a readable silhouette. The intended use should guide the prompt.

Use lighting and style with restraint

Lighting can communicate mood: soft daylight often feels calm, while strong directional light creates contrast. Style terms can guide the result toward an illustration, realistic photograph, or simplified graphic.

Avoid piling on contradictory instructions. Asking for minimalism, extreme detail, dramatic shadows, and a flat design all at once can make the output unpredictable. Choose two or three characteristics that support the message you want the image to convey.

Revise one element at a time

When the first result is close but not quite right, identify the most important difference. Perhaps the subject is too small, the background is distracting, or the lighting does not match the page.

Keep the successful parts of the prompt and change only the relevant instruction. Comparing versions makes it easier to learn which descriptions produce useful results. Save the prompt alongside the final image if you may need a consistent series later.

Check suitability before publishing

Inspect details that may look convincing at a glance but fail under closer examination. Consider whether the image communicates the intended message and whether it could mislead viewers.

For commercial projects, review the image service's usage terms and any applicable rights requirements. Good image prompting is not just about visual quality; it is about creating a useful asset that fits its context.
